VR Virtual Workbench

A WebXR simulation platform supporting both physical VR headsets and traditional flatscreen environments.

Desktop Controls:

  • Left-Click + Drag Background: Orbit scene view
  • Left-Click Objects: Pointer click screens & systems
  • Right-Click + Drag Objects: Slide devices across physical workspace
  • Shift + Raycast Screen: Fast flip/inspect back chassis specs

VR Headset Controls:

  • Pointer Ray + Trigger: Interact with screen/dashboard
  • Controller Proximity + Grip: Grab, slide, or hold phone
  • A or X Button (When holding): Flip physical phone chassis
To modify hardware colors, select a profile in the System controls inside the workspace's floating main dashboard monitor.
